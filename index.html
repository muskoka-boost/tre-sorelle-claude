La nostra storia

Tre Sorelle means “three sisters.”

Tre Sorelle is the family business of sisters Lisa, Gina and Carla. It started simply: homemade pasta, sauce and meatballs sold at the Orillia Farmers' Market.

Family recipes are still at the heart of everything in the case, from fresh pasta and tomato sauce to soups, lasagna and manicotti. Today you'll find the women-owned shop at 37 Mississaga St W in downtown Orillia.
